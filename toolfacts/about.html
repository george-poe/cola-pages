About this fact base

What this is

A small, single-maintainer reference table of AI and creator tools: what each one costs today, what the

free tier actually withholds, and what the vendor's affiliate terms say. It is not a blog, and there are no

opinion pieces about where AI is going.

How every number here was produced

Nobody types prices by hand.

1. A script fetches each vendor's own pricing page and the affiliate program page.

2. The published figure is compared against the text on the vendor page. If the number appears, the page

shows the sentence it was found in, so you can check us.

3. If a figure cannot be matched, the entry is removed from the site rather than softened to

"around $X" or "starts at roughly".

4. This runs weekly. Every page carries the date it was last checked.

That last step is the whole point. Tool reviews go stale in weeks because prices move; a comparison site

that doesn't re-check is publishing archaeology. You may notice a few tools listed in our data table but

absent from the site - those are entries whose pricing we could not verify yet, and we would rather have a

shorter site than a wrong one.

What we do not do

  • No fabricated hands-on experience. If we have not run a tool on a real task, the page says it is based on

the vendor's public page only.

  • No income claims of any kind, and no "make money with AI" advice. Tools do not produce income; someone

with a paying skill does.

  • No sponsored placement disguised as a review. If a page ever carries a paid placement, it will be labelled

on that page.

  • No scraping behind logins, and no automated account creation.

Affiliate links and money

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you pay through one, we may earn a commission; the price to you does

not change. Commission rate, attribution window, payout threshold and payout delay are listed on the pages

that discuss a vendor's program, with the date those terms were checked.

We do not buy our own subscriptions through our own links. Beyond being against the rules of most programs,

it would make the only signal we have - whether a recommendation holds up when you pay for it - meaningless.
